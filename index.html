Media monitoring takes time, and the real risks get lost in the noise.

Scattered sources

News sites, wire services, video platforms and social media are checked one by one; teams spend part of every day searching.

One story, ten headlines

A wire story is republished on dozens of sites; traditional clipping reports list every copy, and the story you read yesterday shows up again today.

Clippings, but no insight

The reader has to work out which stories are positive and which are harmful.

Crises are spotted too late

By the time the report is read, a damaging story has already spread.

Competitors stay invisible

Competitors' wins and setbacks are not tracked systematically.

Yankı does this work for you and brings you only what needs a decision.

Features

More than a clipping service: an analyst that reads your media.

PR Risk alerts

Stories that pose a real reputational threat — allegations, scandals, legal proceedings, negative coverage reaching a wide audience — are flagged in a separate category and shown at the top of the report with a warning sign. Ordinary negative coverage is kept apart from stories that need urgent action.

PR risk watch (Pro and Enterprise)
It does not wait for the scheduled report: it scans frequently in the background and sends an email alert as soon as a PR risk story is found.

Positive Negative Neutral PR Risk

Story-level sentiment analysis

Every story is tagged positive, negative, neutral or PR Risk. Next to the tag you get a short AI summary and a link to the story, so you understand it without reading the full text.

Competitor tracking

News about your competitors is grouped in a separate section and assessed from your point of view as a competitive advantage or a competitive disadvantage.

Duplicate stories merged

The same story published on different sites is grouped into a single card, with every source listed underneath. Your report stays short, and you can see how far the story has spread.

Automatic PDF reports by email

A PDF report is prepared on your schedule and sent automatically to the recipients you choose. Reports can be in English or Turkish and are ready to forward to your board or clients.

Multi-channel scanning

Alongside selected publications, Yankı scans Google News, Bing News, RSS feeds, web search and YouTube. RSS feeds of national news agencies are added to every profile by default; you can add your own sources and filter results by channel.

Bring your own AI key (BYOK)

Analysis runs at the AI provider you choose, with your own key. You control the model and the cost, and you use the provider your organisation has approved.

Flexible scheduling

Set a separate scan frequency for each monitoring profile. Increase it during campaigns or crises, reduce it in quiet periods, or run scans manually only.

Frequently asked questions

Which sources are scanned?+

Reuters, Financial Times, Bloomberg, Bloomberg HT and Dünya are scanned specifically; international publications are searched with English keywords. Google News, Bing News, RSS feeds, web search and YouTube are scanned as well, and you can add your own RSS feeds. Social media channels are considered on request for enterprise plans.

Which AI providers are supported?+

Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI, Google Gemini and any provider with an OpenAI-compatible API.

What is the difference between “PR Risk” and “negative news”?+

Negative news is critical coverage about you. PR Risk is a story that could seriously damage your reputation and needs a quick response, such as allegations, scandals or legal proceedings.

Can I follow an agenda without a brand?+

Yes. Just describe the agenda you want to follow in one sentence, for example “a daily banking agenda for a bank's senior executive”. Yankı suggests the search topics and reports on the agenda at the frequency you choose.

Will the same story appear in every report?+

No. A story that appeared in an earlier report is not reported again. Developing stories are shown with an “Ongoing” label.

How often will I get a report?+

You decide, per monitoring profile. You can schedule automatic scans up to the frequency your plan allows, or run scans manually.

Which languages are reports available in?+

English and Turkish. Story summaries are written in the report language you choose.

Do I need to install anything?+

No. Sign up in your web browser and create your first monitoring profile in minutes.

What are the benefits of using my own AI key?+
  • Transparent costs: AI usage is billed directly at your provider's prices; no hidden AI margin is added to the Yankı subscription.
  • Choice of provider and model: Pick whichever of Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini or an OpenAI-compatible provider fits your needs and budget.
  • Fits your corporate policies: Use the provider your organisation already has a contract with and whose data processing terms it has approved.
  • Full control: Track usage in your provider's console, set spending limits, and rotate or revoke the key at any time.
