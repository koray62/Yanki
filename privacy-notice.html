4. People mentioned in public publications

Yankı only scans content that has been published publicly, for the brands, people and topics defined by customers. Data relating to people named in this content is processed on the customer's instruction and solely for media monitoring and reporting. The customer who decides which people and topics are monitored is responsible for its own data controller obligations for this data; for this processing our company acts as a data processor on the customer's behalf.

Analyses run with your own AI key (BYOK)

News analysis is performed by the AI provider you configure on your account (e.g. Anthropic, OpenAI, Google) using your own API key and account. Content sent for this analysis is processed under your contract with that provider and its data policies. You decide which provider is used.

Transfers abroad rely on the appropriate safeguards set out in KVKK Article 9 and the Regulation on the Procedures and Principles for the Transfer of Personal Data Abroad (e.g. standard contracts published by the Personal Data Protection Board).

6. Retention

Your personal data is kept for as long as the processing purpose requires and for the periods set by law; once this period ends it is erased, destroyed or anonymised. In particular:

  • Account and service data: during the subscription and for 12 months after the subscription ends or the account is closed,
  • Scanned publication content and generated reports: for 6 months from their creation,
  • Invoices and accounting records: for 10 years under the Tax Procedure Law and the Turkish Commercial Code,
  • Demo requests that do not lead to a contract: for 6 months from the request date.

7. Your rights under KVKK Article 11

By applying to our company, you have the right to:

  1. Learn whether your personal data is processed,
  2. Request information if it has been processed,
  3. Learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used in line with that purpose,
  4. Know the third parties in Türkiye or abroad to whom it is transferred,
  5. Request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate,
  6. Request erasure or destruction under the conditions in KVKK Article 7,
  7. Request that third parties to whom data was transferred be notified of actions taken under (e) and (f),
  8. Object to a result against you arising exclusively from analysis by automated systems,
  9. Claim compensation for damages caused by unlawful processing.

8. How to apply

You can submit requests regarding your rights, in line with the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller, in writing to the address above together with information that identifies you, via registered electronic mail (KEP), with a secure electronic or mobile signature, or from the email address registered with us to info@noratech.com.tr.

Your request will be concluded free of charge as soon as possible and within 30 days at the latest, depending on its nature. If the process requires an additional cost, the fee set by the Personal Data Protection Board may be charged.
